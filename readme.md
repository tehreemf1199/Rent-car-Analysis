
1 Project Title
Exploratory Market & Pricing Analysis of Car Rental Platforms

2️ A data-driven analysis of car rental listings to identify demand
patterns, pricing trends, market gaps, rating reliability, and predictive signals using Python.

3️ Business Problem
Business Questions
- Which car makes and models are most popular in different cities?
- What is the typical rental price across major cities?
- Are some cities oversaturated while others show market gaps?
- Are ratings genuinely informative or suspiciously inflated?
- Can rental price or demand be predicted using vehicle and location features?

4️ Dataset Overview 
Dataset Overview
- 5,851 car rental listings
- Includes vehicle details, pricing, ratings, demand metrics, and location data

5️ Approach / Methodology
Approach
- Data cleaning and missing value treatment
- Feature engineering (vehicle age, demand metrics)
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Market segmentation (Oversaturated vs High Potential cities)
- Regression modeling for prediction

6️ Key Findings (MOST IMPORTANT 🔥)
Key Findings
- Vehicle demand varies significantly by city
- Major cities and tourist locations command higher rental prices
- Several cities show high demand but limited supply, indicating market gaps
- Ratings are highly inflated and weak predictors of demand
- Regression models explain pricing and demand moderately (R² ≈ 0.20)

Visual Insights
The notebook includes:

- Market saturation analysis
- Rating distribution patterns
